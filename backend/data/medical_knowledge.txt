Fever with cough and fatigue can occur in viral infections such as influenza, COVID-19, and common cold. Seek urgent care if breathing difficulty, chest pain, confusion, blue lips, or persistent high fever occurs. if you have high fever take paracetomol with medical advice

Chest pain with shortness of breath, sweating, dizziness, or nausea may be a cardiac emergency. Patients should seek emergency medical care immediately.

Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, and fever are common in gastroenteritis or food poisoning. Hydration is important. Urgent care is needed for blood in stool, severe dehydration, severe abdominal pain, or symptoms in infants and elderly patients.

Migraine can cause severe headache, nausea, vomiting, light sensitivity, and dizziness. Sudden worst headache, weakness, speech difficulty, or vision loss requires emergency evaluation.

Dengue can present with high fever, severe headache, rash, joint pain, nausea, and fatigue. Warning signs include abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding, restlessness, and rapid drop in platelets.
